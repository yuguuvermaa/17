# Gugluu weds Gullaa (month 17)

A shaadi card from someday. She pulls the dori, the card opens, and every function is something she does herself.

## Deploy (GitHub Pages)
1. New repo named `seventeen`. The WhatsApp link preview image points to yuguuvermaa.github.io/seventeen/img2.jpeg; if you pick another name, change the `og:image` line in index.html.
2. Upload everything in this folder to the repo root: index.html, the photos (a, b, c, d, f, g, h, i, img2, img6 .jpeg), song.mp3, and the whole `fonts` folder (keep it as a folder named `fonts`).
3. Settings, Pages, Deploy from branch, main, / (root). Wait a minute, then open https://yuguuvermaa.github.io/seventeen/

## Edit words
Everything long she reads is in the `WORDS` object near the bottom of index.html: the four shayaris and their "seedhi baat", the shoot captions, the kundli rows, the mehendi finds, the seven vows, the shagun letter, the RSVP and the ending lines. The invite card text itself is in the HTML near the top of `<main>`.

## What she'll experience
1. A closed maroon card under a marigold toran, tied with a red-gold dori. She taps it, the dori comes undone, the doors swing open, petals fall and the song starts.
2. The invite: Gugluu weds Gullaa, shubh muhurat 01:20 am, every 25th since 25 May 2025.
3. Save the date, with a live counter of "months of rehearsal".
4. A callback to the last line of month 16's book.
5. The pre-wedding shoot: a pile of nine prints she tosses aside one by one.
6. The functions, each one hers to do:
   - Kundli Milan: she taps "milao", the chart fills house by house and the gunas count up to 36/36. The 7th house (marriage) lights last.
   - Haldi: she rubs the turmeric off with her finger to find the two of you cheek to cheek.
   - Mehendi: her hand gets drawn in henna. Six things are hidden in it (gullaa, 01:20, 25, the ananass, the metro, an A in a heart). Each find darkens the mehendi.
   - Saat Phere: she taps the fire, the two of you walk a round tied together, and one vow unlocks per round.
   - Shagun: a red envelope with the extra ₹1 coin. Inside, the letter.
7. RSVP, the ending lines, a countdown to month eighteen, signed gullaa.

Easter egg: open it between 01:20 and 01:29 am IST and the invite notices.
